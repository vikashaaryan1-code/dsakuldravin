## 1. Track A: AI Engineering & Applied Machine Intelligence

### CRS-AI-101: AI Mastery & Prompt Engineering
- **Target Audience:** Students, digital kiosk operators, small business owners.
- **Duration:** 4 Weeks (20 Practical Lab Hours).
- **Module Syllabus:**
  - Chapter 1: Foundations of LLMs & Attention Mechanisms.
  - Chapter 2: The Art of Context Engineering, System Instructions & Few-Shot Prompts.
  - Chapter 3: Structured Data Extraction (JSON/XML) & Function Calling.
  - Chapter 4: Autonomous Coding Agents & Cursor/Windsurf Workflow Mastery.
- **Practical Exercises:** Build an automated citizen inquiry chatbot with bilingual Hindi/English prompt templates.
- **Passing Criteria:** 75% on timed 30-question final exam + 1 end-to-end coding project.

### CRS-AI-102: Generative AI Tools & Enterprise Productivity
- **Duration:** 3 Weeks (15 Practical Lab Hours).
- **Module Syllabus:** Multimodal AI (Text-to-Image, Audio transcription with Whisper, OCR document intelligence).
- **Practical Lab:** Automated citizen document ingestion and formatting into print-ready PDF dockets.

### CRS-AI-103: AI Automation & Agentic Workflows
- **Duration:** 4 Weeks (24 Lab Hours).
- **Module Syllabus:** Webhook triggers, LangChain/LlamaIndex agents, state machines, and multi-agent coordination.
- **Practical Lab:** Automated dispatch of email/WhatsApp notices upon citizen application status changes.

### CRS-AI-104: Practical AI Capstone & Ethical Governance
- **Duration:** 3 Weeks (18 Lab Hours).
- **Module Syllabus:** DPDP 2025 compliance in AI, algorithmic bias prevention, and enterprise deployment.
- **Final Evaluation:** Comprehensive viva + capstone deployment of an automated citizen service assistant.

## 2. Track B: Computer Essentials & Office Systems

### CRS-COMP-101: Computer Fundamentals & Digital Operating Systems
- **Module Syllabus:** Computer hardware architecture, BIOS/UEFI setup, Windows 11 enterprise configuration, peripherals, and secure driver installation.
- **Practical Lab:** Disassembling, cleaning, assembling a desktop workstation, and installing an operating system with dual-stack networking.

### CRS-COMP-102: Windows & Office Productivity Suite
- **Module Syllabus:** Advanced word processing, mail merge for citizen notices, template creation, and PDF mastering.
- **Practical Lab:** Designing official bilingual letterheads, affidavits, and RTI applications.

### CRS-COMP-103: Advanced Excel & Financial Data Management
- **Module Syllabus:** VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, Pivot Tables, Macro basics, GST reconciliation sheets.
- **Practical Lab:** Building a double-entry cash ledger and daily sales reconciliation workbook for an ADCS Kendra.

### CRS-COMP-104: Internet, Cyber Safety & Cloud Storage
- **Module Syllabus:** Network protocols (DNS, IPv4/IPv6, SSL/TLS), phishing defense, password managers, DigiLocker management, and two-factor authentication.
- **Practical Lab:** Hardening workstation browsers against session hijacking and clearing temporary files securely.

## 3. Track C: CCTV & Hardware Infrastructure

### CRS-CCTV-101: CCTV Surveillance Fundamentals & Optics
- **Module Syllabus:** Analog vs IP cameras, focal length, lux ratings, IR night vision, dome vs bullet casings, IP67 weatherproofing.
- **Practical Lab:** Lens selection and field-of-view calculation for a 500 sq ft retail store.

### CRS-CCTV-102: IP Camera & Network Installation
- **Module Syllabus:** CAT6 crimping (T568B standard), PoE switches, IP addressing, subnetting, bandwidth calculation.
- **Practical Lab:** Crimping 4 network cables, configuring static IP addresses on 4 IP cameras, and verifying ping latency.

### CRS-CCTV-103: DVR / NVR Configuration & Storage Sizing
- **Module Syllabus:** H.264 vs H.265 compression, continuous vs motion detection recording, surveillance HDD specs, RAID configurations.
- **Practical Lab:** Configuring motion-triggered recording, email alerts, and calculating 30-day storage requirements for 8 cameras.

### CRS-CCTV-104: Remote Mobile Viewing & Troubleshooting
- **Module Syllabus:** Port forwarding, P2P cloud connection, Hik-Connect / Dahua DMSS mobile setup, multimeter fault tracing, power drop diagnosis.
- **Practical Lab:** Enabling remote mobile live view on Android/iOS devices and resolving video loss on camera 3.

- **Practical Lab:** Drafting a 1-year business profitability plan and monthly break-even projection for a rural kiosk.

## 5. Examination, Grading & Public QR Verification

1. **Proctored Examination Rules:**
   - 60-minute online examination with 40 randomized multiple-choice questions pulled from an active 200-question pool.
   - Passing threshold: **70% minimum score**.
   - Retake cooldown: 48-hour mandatory cooling period before retake eligibility.
2. **Cryptographic Certificate Issuance:**
   - Issued certificate code format: `ADCS-ACAD-2026-{6_DIGIT_SERIAL}`.
   - Every certificate embeds a tamper-evident SHA-256 seal computed from `StudentName + CourseCode + IssueDate + CertificateId`.
   - Accessible on public verification portal: `/verify-certificate/:certificateId`.
