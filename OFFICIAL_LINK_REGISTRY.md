## 2. Statutory Fee Transparency Policy

1. **Govt Services Zero-Fee Rule:** 
   - Services such as Udyam Registration, e-Shram Registration, and Bihar RTPS Caste/Income certificates are statutory free of charge on official government portals.
   - ADCS fee displayed for these services is strictly for **"Technical Guidance, Document Scanning & Assisted Computer Data Entry"** and is never misrepresented as an official government fee.
2. **Itemized Customer Receipt:**
   - Every invoice issued by ADCS explicitly segregates:
     - `Statutory Government Fee (₹)`
     - `ADCS Professional Facilitation Charge (₹)`
     - `Applicable GST (18%) (₹)`
     - `Total Paid by Citizen (₹)`
3. **Audit & Health Check Alert:**
   - The Super Admin Control Tower checks destination URL accessibility weekly. Any destination responding with HTTP 4xx or 5xx triggers a high-priority dashboard flag.
