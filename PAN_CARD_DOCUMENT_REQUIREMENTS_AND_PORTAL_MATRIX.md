## 1. Executive Summary & Legal Framework

Permanent Account Number (PAN) is a ten-digit alphanumeric identifier issued by the Income Tax Department of India under **Section 139A of the Income Tax Act, 1961** and **Rule 114 of the Income Tax Rules, 1962**.

The Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) authorizes two designated service providers to manage PAN applications and data processing across India:
1. **UTIITSL** (*UTI Infrastructure Technology And Services Limited*)
2. **Protean** (*formerly NSDL e-Governance Infrastructure Limited / Protean eGov Technologies Limited*)

Both agencies process statutory applications with identical legal standing. The citizen or business applicant may file either directly on the respective agency portal or through authorized assisted facilitation centers (such as ADCS Kendra / Digital Seva VLE).

## 4. Statutory Document Checklist (Rule 114 Requirements)

An applicant under Form 49A must provide three mandatory proofs:
1. **Proof of Identity (POI)**
2. **Proof of Address (POA)**
3. **Proof of Date of Birth (DOB)**

### 4.1 Proof of Identity (POI) - Accepted Documents
- **Aadhaar Card issued by UIDAI** (*Recommended: Serves as POI, POA & DOB simultaneously*)
- Elector's Photo Identity Card (Voter ID)
- Driving License
- Passport
- Ration card having photograph of the applicant
- Arm's license
- Photo identity card issued by the Central Government or State Government or Public Sector Undertaking
- Pensioner card having photograph of the applicant
- Central Government Health Service (CGHS) Scheme card or Ex-servicemen Contributory Health Scheme (ECHS) card
- Certificate of identity in original signed by a Member of Parliament (MP), Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA), Municipal Councilor, or Gazetted Officer
- Bank certificate on letterhead from branch manager along with photograph and bank account number

### 4.2 Proof of Address (POA) - Accepted Documents
- **Aadhaar Card issued by UIDAI**
- Elector's Photo Identity Card (Voter ID)
- Driving License
- Passport
- Passport of the spouse
- Post office passbook having address of the applicant
- Latest property tax assessment order
- Domicile certificate issued by the Government
- Allotment letter of accommodation issued by Central or State Government (not more than three years old)
- Property registration document
- **Utility Bills** (*Must not be more than 3 months old*):
  - Electricity bill
  - Landline telephone or broadband connection bill
  - Water bill
  - Consumer gas connection card or piped gas bill
  - Bank account statement
  - Depository account (Demat) statement
  - Credit card statement

### 4.3 Proof of Date of Birth (DOB) - Accepted Documents
- **Aadhaar Card issued by UIDAI**
- Elector's Photo Identity Card (Voter ID)
- Driving License
- Passport
- Matriculation / 10th Standard Certificate or Marksheet issued by a recognized board
- Birth certificate issued by Municipal Authority or authorized registrar of births
- Photo identity card issued by Central/State Government, PSU, or Statutory Regulatory Authority
- Domicile certificate issued by the Government
- Central Government Health Service (CGHS) card or ECHS photo card
- Pension payment order
- Marriage certificate issued by Registrar of Marriages
- Affidavit sworn before a magistrate stating the date of birth

## 6. Comprehensive Field Dictionary (Form 49A: A to Z)

| Field No. | Field Label | Mandatory? | Notes & Validation Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | Full Name | **Yes** | Title (Shri / Smt / Kumari / M/s), Last Name (Mandatory), First Name, Middle Name. Single letter initials only allowed in middle name. |
| **02** | Abbreviation on Card | **Yes** | Full name as it will appear on PVC card (Max 35 characters). |
| **03** | Other Names | **Yes** | Have you ever been known by any other name? If Yes, provide former title, first, middle, last name. |
| **04** | Gender | **Yes** | Male / Female / Transgender. Mandatory for individuals. |
| **05** | Date of Birth | **Yes** | DD/MM/YYYY. For Non-Individuals: Date of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / Trust Formation. |
| **06** | Details of Parents | **Yes** | Mother single parent flag (Y/N). Father's name mandatory unless single parent mother. Mother's name optional. Select name on card. |
| **07** | Address - Residence | **Conditional** | Flat/Door, Building, Road, Area, Town/City, State, PIN (6 digits). Required if Communication Address is Residence. |
| **08** | Address - Office | **Conditional** | Name of Office, Flat/Room, Building, Road, Area, City, State, PIN. Required for non-individuals or if communication is Office. |
| **09** | Address for Communication | **Yes** | Residence ('R') or Office ('O'). Determines physical card dispatch destination. |
| **10** | Telephone & Email | **Yes** | Country code (+91), 10-digit mobile number, valid email address for digital e-PAN delivery. |
| **11** | Status of Applicant | **Yes** | Individual, HUF, Company, Firm, Association of Persons, Trust, Body of Individuals, Local Authority, Artificial Juridical Person. |
| **12** | Registration Number | **Conditional** | Company CIN or LLPIN or Registrar of Societies Registration Number (For non-individuals). |
| **13** | Aadhaar Number | **Yes (Ind.)** | 12-digit UIDAI number. Must pass Verhoeff dihedral algorithm checksum. Name on Aadhaar must be provided. |
| **14** | Source of Income | **Yes** | Check at least one: Salary, House Property, Capital Gains, Business/Profession (with code), Other Sources, No Income. |
| **15** | Representative Assessee (RA) | **Conditional** | **Mandatory if Applicant is Minor (<18 years)**, lunatic, or deceased. Requires RA Title, Name, Full Address, and Identity proof. |
| **16** | Assessing Officer (AO) Code | **Yes** | 4-part code: Area Code (3 letters), AO Type (2 letters), Range Code (3 digits), AO No. (2 digits). Derived from City and Ward/Circle. |
| **17** | Documents Enclosed | **Yes** | Exact names of documents chosen for POI, POA, and DOB. |
| **18** | Declaration | **Yes** | Declarant Name, Capacity (Himself/Herself or Representative Assessee), Place, Date. |
